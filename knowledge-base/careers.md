# Careers at Liquid Intelligent Technologies

Source: https://liquid.tech/careers/ (public page, summarised in September 2026).

## Working at Liquid
The careers page describes Liquid as a fast-paced and stimulating place to work that offers good career paths for motivated and skilled people. It says the company operates in one of the fastest-growing markets, where constant change, technology innovation and rapid growth targets are normal, and it looks for people with the right attitude and relevant qualifications.

## Areas the company recruits in
- Sales professionals
- Account management and customer support
- Engineering, networking and project management
- Commercial managers
- Finance, billing and legal
- Information technology
- Marketing and product management

## How to apply
Candidates with relevant experience and a passion for telecommunications are invited to email their interest. The page lists region-specific HR contact addresses for Botswana, Kenya, Mauritius, Tanzania, Uganda, the UK, Zambia and Zimbabwe. South Africa has its own careers portal for current vacancies.

## Open roles
The careers page does not list specific job openings. South African jobseekers are pointed to the South Africa recruitment portal to see current opportunities.
