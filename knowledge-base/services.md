# Liquid Intelligent Technologies: Services and Solutions

Sources: https://liquid.tech/, https://liquid.tech/digital-services/cloud-services/, https://liquid.tech/digital-services/cyber-security/, https://liquid.tech/infrastructure-solutions/ (public pages, summarised in September 2026).

## Overview
Liquid's offering falls into digital services (cloud, managed solutions, cybersecurity, IoT) and infrastructure solutions (connectivity, international wholesale, wholesale products). It also lists data centres, and Microsoft 365 and Azure services.

## Cloud services
Liquid presents its cloud offering around three strengths:
- Flexibility: an elastic cloud model that lets customers scale quickly and on demand.
- Reliability: direct connectivity to the cloud over its resilient, meshed network.
- Security: a secure network and dedicated connections to the cloud to keep data protected.

The cloud services page lists these service groups:
- Productivity: Dynamics 365, Microsoft 365 Plus, Microsoft Copilot, Work From Anywhere (Liquid Virtual Workplace), Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Exchange Online.
- Voice and collaboration: Cloud Contact Centre, Telephone Management System, Teams Business Voice, OneVoice and Unified Communications.
- Cloud connectivity: AWS Direct Connect, Microsoft Azure Peering Service and ExpressRoute (CloudConnect).
- Cloud platforms: Amazon Web Services, Edge Platform-on-Demand, Google Cloud and Azure.
- Other: Professional Services and a "Partner with Liquid" programme.

## Cybersecurity
Based on the cybersecurity content on the site, Liquid provides or describes:
- Cyber Security Fusion Centres: three centres across Africa that add a layer of protection and give access to threat and security data across key markets.
- Education: webinars and workshops to train corporate staff on cyber security.
- Threat assessment and strategy: a full 360-degree cyber security threat assessment for potential clients, with strategies tailored to their needs.
- Information security management: help establishing an Information Security Management System (ISMS) and resilience controls.

The same content stresses employee training and awareness, enterprise-wide security frameworks aligned to business goals, continuous management of technology and systems, and compliance with data protection regulations.

## Infrastructure and connectivity
- Connectivity solutions are the main focus: high-speed broadband and enterprise connectivity across Africa.
- International wholesale is accessed through Liquid Data Port (liquiddataport.com).
- Wholesale products are offered through the infrastructure solutions division.
- Liquid highlights local African expertise and independent ownership, a unified network with faster speeds, more reliability and tighter security, and a diverse product range for businesses of all sizes.
- Customer segments listed: Enterprise, Small Business, Home, Connectivity Partners and Education.
- Related pages cover Enterprise solutions, Small Business solutions and Data Centres.
