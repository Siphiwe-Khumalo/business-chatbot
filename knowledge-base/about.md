# About Liquid Intelligent Technologies

Sources: https://liquid.tech/, https://liquid.tech/about-us/our-story/, https://liquid.tech/about-us/our-network/ (public pages, summarised in September 2026).

## Who they are
Liquid Intelligent Technologies is a business of Cassava Technologies and describes itself as a pan-African technology group. The homepage says it has capabilities across 14 countries, mainly in Sub-Saharan Africa. Its parent, Cassava Technologies, is described on the company story page as a pan-African technology group spanning over 20 countries.

## Purpose, vision and values
- The company's core belief is that every individual on the African continent has the right to be connected.
- Vision: to be the leading digital solutions provider in its chosen markets.
- Mission: a digitally connected future that leaves no African behind.
- Values: faithful stewardship, reliability, creativity and innovation, and trust and respect.

## History
- The organisation began as Econet Satellite Services in 1997.
- It launched its high-speed cross-border fibre network in 2009.
- In 2021 it rebranded as Liquid Intelligent Technologies, the same year Cassava Technologies became its holding company.
- In 2022 it acquired the Telrad Group, which has operations in Israel, Europe and Latin America, as part of a strategy to become a globally operating technology company with African origins.

## What they offer (overview)
The homepage groups the offering into:
- Digital services: cloud services, managed solutions, cybersecurity solutions and IoT.
- Infrastructure solutions: connectivity solutions, international wholesale and wholesale products.
- Other capabilities: data centres, and Microsoft 365 and Azure services.

The story page also mentions cloud and cyber security solutions delivered through global partnerships, digital infrastructure and connectivity, and customised solutions for enterprises and SMEs.

## Network and infrastructure
- Liquid describes its network as Africa's largest independent fibre network, spanning over 116,000 km and established since 2009.
- The network runs the length of the continent, including a direct terrestrial link between Cape Town, South Africa and Cairo, Egypt, described as the first of its kind on the continent.
- It is a self-healing, switched backbone that prioritises traffic within Africa to reduce latency, and it connects to major subsea cable systems.
- It participates in the SAT3/SAFE, WACS, TEAMS and EASSy subsea cable consortia and holds a significant IRU inventory on SEACOM.
- It has direct onward connectivity to international hubs including London and Marseille.
- The company serves over 50 global wholesale carriers across Europe, North America and Asia Pacific.
- Operations span Eastern, Central and Southern Africa.

## Data centres
Liquid has three data centres, in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Nairobi, with combined capacity of 19,000 square metres of rack space and 78 megawatts of power.
